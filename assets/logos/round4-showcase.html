Round 4 Exploration

Recognizable Student Attendance Icons

Moving away from abstract sci-fi geometry to designs that immediately communicate college attendance, timetable roll-call, and the 75% cutoff threshold the instant a student looks at their phone screen.

Concept J — The Attendance Calendar

Timetable Grid + Affirmative Checkmark
A dark titanium calendar card with metallic top hinges and an embossed schedule matrix. An electric emerald 3D checkmark punches forward from the active day cell with affirmative roll-call presence.

Concept K — The 75% Cutoff Gauge

Threshold Meter + Safe Buffer Check
A precision circular attendance gauge on obsidian titanium with an explicit 75% cutoff indicator notch. A vibrant green progress arc wraps safely past the cutoff around a bold centered checkmark.

Concept L — The Bound Student Ledger

Spiral Notebook + Class Status Badge
Plays directly on the name "Bound" as a hardbound student notebook: metallic binder coils on the spine, with a glowing green attendance checkmark badge and daily streak indicator dots on the cover.
